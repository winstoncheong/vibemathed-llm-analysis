What this is

VibeMathed (vibemathed.com) is a community record of precisely stated open problems now proved or disproved with an AI model substantively in the loop. This page scans its full public /api/dataset and counts which vendors and models show up, broken down by how much the AI contributed, how the result was checked, and how significant the problem was. Re-run the three scripts in this repo to refresh every number below. Shares are \(s = 100 \cdot w / n\) with \(n = 1284\).

1. Coverage

1284 tracked problems, and vendor attribution is near-complete: all but 30 (2.3%) name the lab or model behind the solve. Status is a separate axis: 517 fully resolved (40.3%), 520 candidates under review, 225 partial. Contribution tiers: AI-discovered 894, co-developed 251, assisted 136. Verification is thin: 708 unreviewed, 318 Lean-checked, 159 Lean-verified, and only 14 independently expert-verified.

3. Significance

  • OpenAI dominates volume. 1127 of 1284 problems (87.8%) involve OpenAI; Anthropic is next at 195 (15.2%). The October 2026 bulk release alone contributes 497 entries.
  • Volume sits at low significance. OpenAI's mean significance is 27.7 (median 25.0); Harmonic's is 14.6 with a median of 10 — the low end driven by bulk sweeps of machine-generated conjectures. The heaviest entries are predominantly OpenAI candidates still under review (see table).
  • Verification lags far behind claims. 708 of 1284 entries (55.1%) are unreviewed by anyone independent; the top of the significance table is candidates, not verified resolutions.
  • Open weights are nearly absent. 6 of 1284 problems involve an open-weights model at all, and only as a co-contributor:
  • colombo-s-difference-power-determinant-conjecture
  • critical-site-percolation-z-d
  • dean-s-conjecture-for-k-5
  • equivalence-of-generic-stability-notions-for-keisler-measures
  • quartic-hessian-conjecture-dimension-four
  • ziegler-cross-polytope-01
  • Anthropic's mix is more collaborative. 66 of its 195 problems (34%) are AI co-developed with named human collaborators, against 149 of 1127 (13%) for OpenAI — whose count is dominated by AI-discovered bulk-release entries.

4. Caveats

  • Vendor attribution mixes a semi-structured modelMaker field with regex mining of free-text model descriptions (PROV_PATTERNS in analyze_vibemathed.py); matched spans are kept per problem as spans in the analysis JSON for audit. "Unreleased internal" labels are attributed to their named lab.
  • Significance scores are AI-estimated curator judgments (Riemann = 100), stable to a band of about five points — treat one-point gaps as ties.
  • Candidates are claims, not results: 520 of the resolutions counted on the site's own stats page are still under review. This page counts problems, not truths.
